# Supplement Counseling Rules

## Product position

Supplements are a dedicated encyclopedia branch, not a subsection of the general drug-family list.

The branch is organized into:

- Vitamins
- Minerals
- Pediatric supplements
- Combination products
- Growth / amino-acid products
- Safety review

## Required practical fields

Every verified supplement profile should answer:

1. Exact ingredient and formulation/salt.
2. What amount on the label represents the clinically relevant ingredient (for example elemental iron, calcium, magnesium or zinc).
3. How to take it.
4. With food, without food, or product-specific.
5. Whether a fat-containing meal matters.
6. Important separation from medicines, foods or other minerals.
7. Whether splitting the daily amount improves absorption or tolerability.
8. Important formulation differences.
9. Common actionable adverse effects.
10. Who needs monitoring or laboratory testing.
11. Patient-facing Arabic counseling.
12. Focused printable patient card.

## Do not generalize across salts

Examples:

- Calcium carbonate: take with food.
- Calcium citrate: can be taken with or without food.
- Magnesium gluconate: take with meals to reduce GI effects.
- Other magnesium salts require their own verified profile.
- Oral iron salts are counseled by elemental iron and are best absorbed on an empty stomach, but food may be used when GI intolerance limits adherence.

## Vitamin D3

Dietary fat improves vitamin D absorption. Patient wording should be practical:

خذ الجرعة مع وجبة أو سناك يحتوي قليلًا من الدهون ليساعد الجسم على امتصاصه بشكل أفضل.

Milk is not mandatory. Do not tell patients that vitamin D must be taken with milk.

## Patient-facing language

The Patient tab is Arabic, simple and action-based.

The first visible card should usually be:

كيف آخذه؟

Then show only relevant points such as:

- timing,
- separation from other products,
- common manageable problem,
- important warning,
- duplication risk.

## Print card

The print card is shorter than the Patient tab and is generated from the same structured counseling source so the two cannot silently contradict each other.

## Evidence rule

Food timing, salt/formulation differences, elemental-content rules, interactions and monitoring requirements must be verified from current authoritative references before a profile is marked production-ready.
